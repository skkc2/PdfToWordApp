Offline PDF to Word (.docx)

Hybrid conversion: editable text first, with an optional visual reference appendix. Your PDF stays on this device.

Conversion options

Select a PDF to begin.

Text, approximate font styling and line positions are reconstructed into editable Word paragraphs. Complex layouts, tables, logos and shapes may not be reconstructed exactly. The appendix preserves source-page appearance as images. Scanned PDFs need OCR for editable text; OCR is not included. Limits: 30 MB, 300 pages.

Offline readiness will be checked by your browser.
